Regions are topics; in a cluster's claims (open a cluster, then "See its claims"), dashed regions inside them are clusters (close Ideas, found automatically). An item or claim with several topics is shown in one of them. Position inside a region is for reading, not causality. Arrows point from the later claim to the one it relates to. In Topics, a line between clusters or topics is solid when relations or shared Ideas back it, dashed when only similarity does; thicker is stronger.
